Privacy Policy & WhatsApp Data Governance

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Version 1.0

At Utsav (operated as utsav-ai, accessible at https://utsav-ai.netlify.app), we respect the privacy of celebration hosts, families, and invited guests. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of personal information, with specific focus on WhatsApp-native guest coordination and Meta WhatsApp Business API compliance.

Summary of Core Commitment: Utsav never sells your personal data, never uses guest phone numbers for third-party marketing, and honors opt-outs (STOP) unconditionally at the send layer. All guest data can be purged on demand.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver deterministic event planning, budget rescaling, and guest concierge services, we collect:

  • Host Profile Information: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, preferred celebration traditions, and geographic city/pincode.
  • Guest Contact Lists: Names, mobile phone numbers (E.164 normalized), family/friend affiliation, headcount, dietary preferences (e.g. Vegetarian, Jain), and accessibility needs (e.g. wheelchair access) uploaded by the event host.
  • Event Operational Logistics: Multi-day sub-event schedules, venue addresses, parking instructions, dress codes, and ceremonial timings.
  • Vendor & Budget Data: Itemized budget allocations, uploaded vendor quotation documents, and contract milestones.

2. How We Use WhatsApp Messaging (Meta API Compliance)

Utsav utilizes the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API exclusively for transactional, event-related coordination requested by the event host.

  • Digital Invitations: Delivering interactive invitation cards with one-tap RSVP buttons (Attending / Decline / Maybe).
  • Live Ceremony Reminders: Automated notifications sent at verified intervals (7 days before, 1 day before, and morning-of) informing attendees of venue arrival and timing details.
  • Fact Sheet Grounded Concierge: Answering guest questions strictly from verified event fact sheets (such as venue location, valet parking, and attire recommendations).

Strict Answering Boundary: The automated guest concierge bot answers questions exclusively from the host-verified event fact sheet. It is technologically barred from disclosing private financial details, vendor contract terms, or unrelated personal information.

3. Guest Consent & Instant Opt-Out Policy (STOP Keyword)

We strictly adhere to Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policies and telecommunications anti-spam standards:

  • Host-Initiated Consent: Hosts confirm prior consent from family and acquaintances before importing contact rosters.
  • Unconditional Opt-Out: Any guest may reply with STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, HALT, or ROKO at any time.
  • Send-Layer Enforcement: When an opt-out keyword is received, the phone number is immediately added to an immutable opt_outs blocklist. All future automated outbound transmissions to that number are blocked before reaching Meta's servers.

4. Data Retention & Right to Erasure (PII Purge)

We practice strict data minimization:

  • Right to Immediate Erasure: An event host can permanently purge any guest's record at any time through the workspace interface or via the direct API endpoint DELETE /api/v1/events/{id}/guests/{guest_id}.
  • Post-Event Purge: Guest contact lists are retained only during the active planning and execution lifecycle of the event. Hosts may request complete archival or erasure post-celebration.

5. Security & Third-Party Service Providers

We protect your data through industry-standard safeguards:

  • Encryption: All web traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. All Meta webhooks require X-Hub-Signature-256 HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
  • Infrastructure Partners:
    • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Processes WhatsApp message delivery under Meta's Cloud API Data Processing Terms.
    • Render Services, Inc.: Hosts backend compute and managed PostgreSQL databases in high-security data centers (Singapore).
    • Netlify, Inc.: Delivers frontend edge caching and static web delivery.
