# Brag Plan: Utsav (उत्सव)

## What is this app?
Utsav (उत्सव) is a culture-native GenAI event orchestration platform for Indian celebrations that blends multimodal design intelligence with deterministic math—so dates, muhurats, and budgets never hallucinate.

## The angle
Indian wedding planning is notoriously chaotic—filled with spreadsheet errors, messy vendor PDFs, and runaway budgets. Generic AI models invent fake muhurat dates and mangle rupee arithmetic. Utsav delivers rock-solid cultural fidelity: multimodal aesthetic extraction via Google Gemini, deterministic panchang & muhurat calculations, and pixel-perfect Devanagari typography.
